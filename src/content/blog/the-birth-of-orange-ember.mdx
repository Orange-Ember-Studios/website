## A Vision Sparked

On **April 1st, 2026**, a new spark was ignited in the digital world. Orange Ember Studios was born not just as a company, but as the fulfillment of a lifelong dream: a dedicated space where video games and high-end software are forged with the same obsessive level of craftsmanship.

I've always believed that the line between a great game and a great piece of software is thinner than most think. Both require a soul, a purpose, and a flawless user experience.

## What We Forge

At Orange Ember, we operate at the intersection of three fundamental pillars:

1.  **Technological Research:** Constantly investigating emerging paradigms to keep our tools at the cutting edge.
2.  **Interactive Entertainment:** Developing immersive desktop and mobile games that respect the player's time and intelligence.
3.  **Digital Products:** Crafting web platforms that are as performant as they are beautiful.

## Our Technological DNA

To achieve our vision, we've carefully curated a stack that allows for speed without sacrificing power. 

### The Game Forge
Our game development is powered by **Godot 4.x** using **GDScript**. We've chosen Godot for its lightweight architecture and excellent open-source community. For physics that feel real and stable, we integrate **Godot Jolt**.

### The Web & Mobile Craft
For the web, we love the flexibility of **Astro** paired with **React** or **Vue**, styled with **Tailwind CSS**. Our infrastructure lives on **Cloudflare** for global speed, with **Supabase** providing a robust, scalable backend.

*   **Mobile:** We use **React Native** to deliver high-performance cross-platform experiences.
*   **Backends:** Depending on the mission, we utilize **Node.js**, **Golang**, or **C++** for high-performance cores.

## A Global Commitment

From day one, Orange Ember is built to be international. We are committed to supporting **English, Spanish, and French** in everything we create. We believe that language should never be a barrier to an extraordinary digital experience.

This is just the beginning. The ember is glowing, and the forge is hot. 

**Welcome to Orange Ember Studios.**
