## Slicing for the Perfect Percentage

At **Orange Ember Studios**, we always look for mechanics that seem simple at first glance but hide technical and addictive depth. This is how **Exact Slice** was born—our latest project developed in Godot 4.6.

The premise is straightforward: you have a pizza, and you must cut it to get a specific percentage. Sounds easy? Try getting a perfect 12.5% with a single stroke while the timer is ticking.

## The Geometric Challenge in Godot 4.6

From a development standpoint, creating a robust 2D slicing system required advanced polygon handling. We used `Polygon2D` and `CollisionPolygon2D` to represent the pizzas, allowing each player's stroke to recalculate the resulting shapes in real-time.

### Powered by Jolt Physics

Although Exact Slice is primarily a 2D experience, we've integrated **Jolt Physics** to manage area detections and collisions with millimeter precision. This ensures that no matter how complex the cutting angle, the engine responds flawlessly.

## Variety and Customization: The Skins

A pizza game wouldn't be complete without a good variety of toppings. We've implemented a robust economy and customization system that allows players to unlock different styles:
- **Cosmic:** A pizza dusted with stardust.
- **Royal:** For the most sophisticated tastes, with golden crusts.
- **Frozen:** Straight from the freezer, with frosty visual effects.

Each skin is more than just a texture swap; thanks to our custom shader system and circular masks, the visual feedback when slicing feels unique for every style.

## Polish and Game Feel

To make the game feel *Premium*, we focused heavily on micro-animations. When you make a cut:
1. The pieces smoothly separate with a **Tween** effect.
2. A bright flash highlights the cut line.
3. The percentage is calculated and displayed with clean, vibrant typography.

It's that combination of mathematical precision and visual satisfaction that defines the **Exact Slice** experience.
