## From Concept to Reality

Developing **Inverse Pulse** has been one of the most exciting journeys for us at Orange Ember Studios. We set out to create an addictive endless runner inspired by classics like *Flappy Bird*, but with a crucial twist: a gravity inversion mechanic wrapped in a stunning cyberpunk aesthetic.

Today, I want to share how we transitioned from a basic prototype in Godot 4 to a polished product, highlighting the challenges we conquered along the way.

## Defying Gravity in a Cyberpunk World

One of the defining features of **Inverse Pulse** is its core gameplay loop. Instead of simply jumping, the player taps to invert gravity, shooting their avatar upwards or downwards to avoid obstacles. Balancing the physics so it feels responsive and fair took continuous iteration. 

Visually, we leaned heavily into a neon-soaked cyberpunk style. The vibrant visual feedback, paired with procedurally generated audio layers and dynamic electronic sound effects, provides an immersive, high-energy arcade feel. 

## Visual Polish and the Shop UI

A great game isn't just about core mechanics; it's also about its surrounding ecosystem. We spent serious effort refining our user interfaces. Perfecting the layout by centering the product grid in the shop modal might seem like a minute detail, but it's exactly this level of symmetry and aesthetic polish that delivers the *premium* experience defined by Orange Ember values. 

## Global Competition: Google Play Games

To boost replayability and community engagement, integrating *Google Play Games Services* was a critical milestone. Guaranteeing that the plugin initialized correctly and that scores were reliably reported to global leaderboards on Android devices posed intriguing challenges. By ironing out authentication behaviors and API synchronization, we successfully implemented the competitive vision for the game.

## Available Now

Inverse Pulse continues to evolve, and we're thrilled to watch the community master the leaderboards.
